How It Works
  1. Sign in. Enter the cell phone number or email address the school has on file for you, then type the 6-digit code we send you.
  2. Choose what you need from the buttons at the top of your home screen:
    • Report an Absence — send an excused note from home with your signature (up to 5 per semester, within 5 school days of the absence), or upload a photo or PDF of a doctor's note.
    • Change Dismissal — change how your child goes home in the afternoon (car line, bus, after care, walker or student driver). Same-day changes must be in by 1:00 PM.
    • Pick-Up People — add or remove people allowed to pick up your child.
  3. Submit. The front office reviews it, and you can check its status anytime under History.

Not finding your info? Call the front office at (904) 730-5500.

Excused Note from Home

Before You Submit
  1. You may submit up to 5 parent excused notes per student each semester (18 weeks). After that, a doctor's note is needed for an absence to be excused.
  2. Submit the note within 5 school days of the absence.
  3. One note per day absent. If your student missed several days, submit a note for each day.
